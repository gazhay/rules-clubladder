# Privacy & Cookie Statement

This privacy notice explains how Club Ladder collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use the Club Ladder website at [cycleracing.club](https://cycleracing.club/).

Our website supports organised cycling competitions, including rider rankings, team and individual competitions, race results and performance verification.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## Our contact details

For questions about this privacy notice or how we use your personal information, contact us at:

**Email:** privacy@cycleracing.club

## What information we collect, use and why

We collect and process personal information to administer competitions, calculate rider rankings, produce race results and ensure fair and consistent competition.

### Information you provide directly

When you create and maintain an account on Club Ladder, we may collect:

- Your name and account details.
- Your email address and contact information.
- Your team, club and competition participation details.
- Your competition preferences and other information you choose to provide.

### Information obtained from Zwift

Club Ladder uses Zwift's account authorisation mechanism to obtain permission to access information associated with your Zwift account.

When you authorise Club Ladder, you permit us to retrieve relevant information made available through Zwift's services and APIs.

Depending on the information made available by Zwift and required for competition administration, this may include:

- Zwift account identifiers and profile information.
- Rider names and profile details.
- Physical measurements and performance-related information.
- Cycling activities and associated performance data.
- Race results, times, power data and other activity metrics.

This access is not limited to the completion of an individual race. While your Zwift authorisation remains active, Club Ladder may periodically retrieve relevant information to maintain rider rankings, assess eligibility and undertake competition-related checks.

We may retrieve and examine information before, during and after competitions, as necessary for the administration and verification of racing activities.

### Information obtained from other sources

Where applicable, we may also obtain competition and performance information from other services, including ZwiftPower and ZwiftRacing, or from information submitted directly by riders, team captains or event organisers.

We only use information from these sources where it is relevant to competition administration, results or performance verification.

### Information we generate through analysis

We also generate information by analysing the data collected from your account and racing activities. This may include:

- Rider rankings and ladder positions.
- Performance comparisons and historical performance trends.
- Race eligibility and verification outcomes.
- Competition results and standings.
- Flags or observations identifying potential irregularities or rule violations.

These calculations and assessments form part of the administration and integrity of Club Ladder competitions.

## How we use your information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

- To create and maintain your Club Ladder account.
- To associate your Zwift account with your Club Ladder membership.
- To calculate rider rankings, ladder positions and competition standings.
- To assess eligibility for competitions and appropriate competition divisions.
- To retrieve and examine performance information before, during and after races.
- To verify race results and investigate potential irregularities or breaches of competition rules.
- To maintain historical race results and competition records.
- To administer teams, clubs and other competition-related activities.
- To communicate with members about their participation and competition administration.
- To maintain the security, reliability and proper operation of the website.

## Automated processing and performance verification

Club Ladder uses automated calculations and data analysis to determine rider rankings, assess performance and identify potential inconsistencies in racing data.

These processes may compare information across multiple activities and races to help identify unusual performance patterns or potential breaches of competition rules.

Automated checks are used to assist competition administration and verification. Where a matter requires a consequential decision, such as a potential disqualification or other competition sanction, it is subject to the applicable competition rules and human review.

## Our lawful bases for processing

We process personal information under the lawful bases provided by the UK GDPR.

Depending on the particular processing activity, these may include:

**Legitimate interests**

We have a legitimate interest in administering Club Ladder competitions, maintaining accurate rankings and results, ensuring fair competition, verifying performances and preventing abuse of competition rules.

We consider this processing necessary and proportionate to the operation and integrity of the competitions.

**Consent**

Where we rely on consent for a particular processing activity, we will explain what information is involved and the purpose for which it is used.

You may withdraw consent where consent is the applicable lawful basis.

**Contract**

Where relevant, we may process information where it is necessary to provide the services and competition participation arrangements you have requested or agreed to.

## Where we obtain personal information

We obtain personal information from:

- You directly, when you create and maintain your Club Ladder account.
- Zwift, through the account authorisation granted by you.
- Other third-party cycling and competition services, where applicable.
- Team captains, race organisers or other authorised competition administrators.
- Our own calculations and analysis of previously collected competition and performance information.

## Zwift account authorisation and revocation

Your Zwift account authorisation allows Club Ladder to access relevant information made available through Zwift's services.

You can revoke this authorisation through the relevant Zwift account settings or available authorisation controls.

If you revoke access, Club Ladder will no longer be able to retrieve new information through that authorisation.

Revoking access does not automatically delete information previously collected or historical race results. Previously collected information may continue to be retained and used where there is an appropriate lawful basis to do so, including maintaining competition records and historical results.

Where access is required for competition participation, withdrawing that access may affect your eligibility to participate in competitions or have your results verified.

## How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

Information relating to current accounts, competition participation and rider rankings is retained for as long as necessary to administer the competitions.

Historical race results and competition records may be retained for longer periods to preserve the history and integrity of Club Ladder competitions.

We do not intend to retain personal information indefinitely where it is no longer necessary. Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

## Sharing information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share relevant information where necessary with:

- Zwift and other relevant services where required for account authorisation or competition administration.
- Website hosting, infrastructure and technical service providers who help us operate Club Ladder.
- Authorised competition administrators and team officials where necessary for competition management and verification.
- Relevant parties where required by law or to protect the integrity of competitions.

Information included in published race results, rankings and competition standings may be visible to other website users and the public.

We only share information that is relevant and necessary for the applicable purpose.

## Sharing information outside the UK

Some of our website infrastructure and service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will ensure that the transfer complies with applicable UK data protection legislation and that appropriate safeguards are in place where required.

Further information about international transfers can be requested using the contact details above.

## Minors

Club Ladder competitions may have minimum age requirements specified by the relevant competition rules.

We take particular care when processing personal information relating to Minors (persons under 16 years of age.)

Where we process information relating to Minors, we will ensure that an appropriate lawful basis exists and that any additional requirements relating to that persons personal information are met.

Where consent is relied upon for an online service offered directly to Minors, the applicable age and authorisation requirements will be observed.

If you have concerns about information relating to a Minor, please contact us using the details above.

## Your data protection rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, you have rights including:

**Right of access** – You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

**Right to rectification** – You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.

**Right to erasure** – You can request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances.

**Right to restriction of processing** – You can ask us to restrict how we use your information in certain circumstances.

**Right to object** – You can object to processing based on legitimate interests in certain circumstances.

**Right to data portability** – You can request that information you provided to us be transferred to you or another organisation in certain circumstances.

**Right to withdraw consent** – Where we rely on consent as our lawful basis, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

These rights are subject to certain conditions and exemptions under data protection legislation.

You do not normally have to pay a fee to exercise your rights.

If you make a request, we will normally respond within one calendar month.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details provided at the beginning of this notice.

# Cookie Policy

A cookie is a small file stored on your device by a website. Cookies can be used to maintain website sessions, remember preferences and support website functionality.

You can manage or disable cookies through your internet browser settings, although disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the website from functioning correctly.

### Cookies used by Club Ladder

We currently use the following functional cookie:

**Cookie key:** connect.ssid

**Expiration:** Session

**Purpose:** Maintains your authenticated website session, allowing the server to recognise your account and maintain your login state as you navigate between pages.

This cookie is necessary for the operation of the website and is not used for advertising or tracking purposes.

We will update this policy if our use of cookies or similar technologies changes.
